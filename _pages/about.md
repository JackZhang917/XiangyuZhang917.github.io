I'm Xiangyu Zhang (I go by Jack), a PhD student in the [Liu Group](#) in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Pittsburgh, advised by Prof. Peng Liu. My research sits at the interface of computational chemistry and machine learning, spanning quantum chemistry calculations (Gaussian 16, DFT), classical and ab initio molecular dynamics, energy decomposition analysis, machine learning force fields, and hybrid ML/MM methods — my current focus.

I received my B.S. in Chemistry from the College of Chemistry and Molecular Sciences at Wuhan University, where I worked with Prof. Xiaotian Qi. Between undergrad and my PhD, I was an AI-for-Science intern at the Shanghai AI Laboratory (上海人工智能实验室), where I helped build a molecular and chemical-reaction database. I'm also a Teaching Assistant for the undergraduate Organic Chemistry Laboratory course at Pitt.

I'm interested in interdisciplinary work at the boundary of AI/ML and chemistry, in academia or industry.
